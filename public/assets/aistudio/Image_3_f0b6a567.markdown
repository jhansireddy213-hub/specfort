## Brand & Style

This design system serves higher education faculty, curriculum review boards, and accreditation bodies navigating high-stakes academic governance. It balances the systematic efficiency of an issue tracker with vibrant, striking typography and structured editorial layouts.

The emotional baseline is energetic, modern, and rigorous. Faculty navigating multi-stage approval workflows, syllabus versioning, and learning outcome matrices experience high-impact visual feedback and bold typographic hierarchy. Visual styling adheres to a vibrant corporate-editorial paradigm: deliberate whitespace, crisp hairline structural borders, flat structural planes, high-contrast typography, and explicit tabular data presentation. 

## Colors

The palette establishes an academic environment through high-contrast legibility, cool-tinted structural neutrals, and unambiguous semantic status indicators, accented by vibrant cyan interactive elements (`#47f0f6`) and bold secondary magenta accents (`#ff0dde`).

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Canvas Background**: `#f8fafc` (Cool slate-tinted canvas preventing ocular strain under sustained reading)
- **Surface / Panel Background**: `#ffffff` (Pure white for elevated cards, document sheets, and data tables)
- **Primary Interactive / Accent**: `#47f0f6` (Vibrant cyan for brand identity, primary actions, focused tabs, and key navigational landmarks)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#ff0dde` (For secondary metadata, supporting labels, and sub-navigation controls)
- **Tertiary Accent**: `#0a8cdc` (For high-priority highlights and interactive accents)
- **Structural Border Hairline**: `#e2e8f0` (Crisp 1px definition for cards, splitters, tables, and form controls)

## Typography

The type system is built on **Anton** for headlines, **Anybody** for body text, and **Archivo Narrow** for labels, configured for academic precision and visual impact.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a structured workbench composition with a persistent 260px collapsible institutional navigation sidebar on the left, an optional contextual document outline tree (280px) on the inner left, a fluid main document canvas with a reading boundary maximum of 1140px, and an optional 320px metadata/audit sidebar on the right.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through flat, architectural surface layering and high-precision boundary hairlines rather than diffused drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design uses a clean, disciplined geometric radius strategy that mirrors legal and institutional documentation.

## Components

Components leverage the new vibrant accent colors, robust typography, and clean layout spacing to deliver an exceptional user experience across all academic workflows.
